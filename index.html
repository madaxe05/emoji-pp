Emoji Battery Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how the Emoji Battery app for Android (package com.madaxe.sohan.emojibattery, "the app") handles information. The app is published by Madaxe ("we", "us"). If you have a question, email dhungel9814@gmail.com.

The short version
  • There is no account and no sign-in. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your battery information and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which can also fill ads from its partner Unity Ads. These companies may use your advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it.
  • Anonymous crash reports and usage counts may be sent to Google Firebase so we can fix bugs.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in the app: Settings, then Privacy choices.

Information that stays on your phone

To work, the app reads and saves the following on your device only. We never receive it.

  • Battery level, charging state and, where your phone reports them, battery temperature and voltage. These drive the mascot moods, the charging animation, alerts and widgets.
  • Your choices: characters, charging animation, overlay position and size, screen mascot settings, alert thresholds, widget styles and the app theme.
  • Small counters the app needs to run, such as when the next ad is due, how many ads you watched toward unlocking each mascot, your daily visit streak and daily gift, how fond each mascot is of you, your language choice and when the app last asked for a rating.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings, Apps, Emoji Battery, Storage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows full-screen ads (app open, interstitial and rewarded ads) served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect or receive your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or a region with similar rules, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are used. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings, Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ad partner: Unity Ads. AdMob may fill some interstitial and rewarded ads through Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). When it does, Unity may receive the same kinds of information (advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions) to deliver, measure and, if you allow it, personalize ads. Your consent choices from the app are passed on to Unity. See the Unity Privacy Policy for app users.

Rewarded ads. Some mascots unlock after you choose to watch a few rewarded ads. Watching ads to unlock is optional, and your unlock progress is stored only on your phone.

US privacy rights. If you live in a US state with consumer privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted ads in the app under Settings, Privacy choices (when available for your region), or by turning off ad personalization in your phone's settings.

Crash reports and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

The app may use Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Crashlytics sends crash details (device model, Android version, app version and the error that occurred) so we can fix problems. Analytics sends anonymous events such as "charging animation turned on" so we know which features are used. These reports do not include your battery history, and we cannot use them to identify you. See the Firebase privacy information.

Ratings

Now and then the app may show Google Play's own rating window. Your rating and review go directly to Google Play under Google Play's terms; the app never sees them.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, messages, files or precise location. We don't sell personal information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

  • On-device data: until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Ad data: kept by Google and Unity under their own retention rules.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase for up to 90 days.

Security

The Google services the app uses send data over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Everything else stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

Your choices and rights

You can turn off features, withdraw ad consent in Privacy choices, reset your advertising ID, or uninstall the app at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. We don't keep your personal information on our own servers, but email us and we will help, including with requests about data held by Google or Unity.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
